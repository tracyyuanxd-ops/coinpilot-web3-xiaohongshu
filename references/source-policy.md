# Source policy

## Evidence order

Use sources in this order:

1. Project or protocol documentation, official announcements, governance proposals, and repositories
2. Original technical standards, research papers, audit reports, regulator publications, and legislation
3. Multiple reputable industry publications for independent context or cross-checking
4. Educational platforms and videos for topic discovery and beginner-language inspiration

Return to levels 1–2 to verify regulation, market figures, yields, token mechanics, protocol status, security claims, and dates.

## User-provided inspiration pool

- Binance Academy: https://www.binance.com/en/academy
- PANews series: https://www.panewslab.com/zh/series
- Simplilearn, *Blockchain in 7 Minutes*: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yubzJw0uiE4

Use these to discover beginner questions and content directions. Do not copy their wording or rely on one secondary source for a material claim.

## Citation rules

- Provide the source name, direct URL, and publication or update date.
- If no date is visible, write `页面未注明日期；访问于 YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Prefer a direct document or announcement over a homepage or search-result link.
- Paraphrase. Do not reproduce long passages.
- Note when a conclusion is an inference rather than an explicit source claim.
- Cross-check facts that may have changed since publication.

## Topic acceptance test

Accept a topic only if it answers a real beginner question, can be supported by reliable sources, and adds a new concept or angle to recent work. Reject price-only stories, calls to trade, airdrop speculation, unsupported rumors, and jargon without reader value.
